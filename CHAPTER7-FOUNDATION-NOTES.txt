CHAPTER 7 FRAMEWORK — LOCAL ONLY, 17 November 2026

Prepared code is not a completed staff-access rollout. Nothing here has been
deployed or enabled in the production app.

WHAT IS PREPARED
1. Memberships and configurable per-business plans. Existing creators become
   owners. No plan is seeded; 1 user/250 products and 2 users/500 products remain
   provisional SME examples. Missing plans never mean unlimited.
2. Server member/product caps serialize concurrent additions. Bulk operations
   roll back completely if over limit. Existing records remain editable/removable
   after a downgrade; no automatic deletions. Explicit null means unlimited.
   Product counts include all rows in the business; active memberships use seats.
3. Configurable admin/manager/staff capabilities, with missing settings denied.
   Active owners retain capability access and exclusively configure role powers.
   No staff preset, order-approval workflow or permission to share is assumed.
4. New businesses automatically get their recorded creator as their first owner.
   This first seat may precede plan selection; extra seats require a plan.
   Null creators are left unclaimed, never assigned to the current caller.
5. Prepared read-only business-access adapter, advisory capacity helper and a
   v2 retry journal scoped to user AND business. None is wired into a switcher.
   Legacy v1 journals are retained and blocked from automatic reassignment.

LOCAL EVIDENCE
verify-chapter7-local.ps1 restores public schema only, stubs auth locally, and
seeds synthetic businesses/products. It tests membership isolation, revocation,
API write denial, owner bootstrap, role configuration, default denial and quotas.
Real two-connection tests pass for members AND products at READ COMMITTED and
REPEATABLE READ: exactly one competing addition wins the last available slot.
Tests also cover batch rollback, downgrade/edit/delete, explicit unlimited and
missing plans. Chapter6 and Chapter7 read-only preflight scripts were exercised.
Business access/capacity/session recovery/scoped-journal tests and TypeScript pass.
Local auth stubs do NOT prove hosted authentication or physical phone acceptance.

Before deployment: authorize the target, verify a fresh backup, inspect live
creators/ownership/default grants/RLS, and reconcile partial objects. Do not rerun
CREATE migrations blindly. Legacy null creators and unassigned products need an
explicit resolution plan. Owner/business deletion also needs a coordinated path
because membership/entitlement references deliberately restrict deletion.
Never drop populated tables or delete data to bring a downgraded plan under quota.

prepared authorization predicate, not automatically applied to existing tables.
Prepare business-scoped read/write APIs and RLS for every workflow, including
child-record ownership and separate cost visibility. Connect scoped queries,
cache invalidation and journal/RPC business identity together before a switcher.
Add invitations/member management with trusted server-selected roles, target
identity checks, expiry/replay protection and atomic capacity enforcement.
Provision explicit plans; do not silently assign provisional prices or limits.
Connect owner-controlled capability configuration and choose user-friendly role
presets later. Staff presets/PO approval are activation decisions, not a blocker
to continuing the configurable backend framework.
The v2 journal needs a coordinated business-scoped RPC rollout and legacy recovery
UI. Its caller must retain single-writer save coordination; it is not a new RPC.
No billing, service purchase, external invitation or production data change.
